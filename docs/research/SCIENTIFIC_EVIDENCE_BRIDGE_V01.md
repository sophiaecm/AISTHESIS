## Purpose

Scientific Evidence Bridge v0.1 connects the AISTHESIS Mini-Lab
scientific-document pipeline to the existing Common Evidence
architecture.

Pipeline:

ScientificDocumentState
→ ScientificStructureState
→ ScientificRelationshipGraph
→ ScientificEvidenceBridge
→ EvidenceBundle
→ CommonEvidenceState

The bridge transports scientific-document information into the
existing evidence architecture without converting extracted or
inferred content into scientific truth.

---

## Core Epistemic Boundary

The following distinctions are mandatory:

content reported in a paper != observed physical fact

reported result != reproduced result

author claim != established fact

hypothesis candidate != supported hypothesis

relationship candidate != verified scientific relationship

SUPPORTS relationship != demonstrated scientific support

CONTRADICTS relationship != demonstrated scientific contradiction

AISTHESIS inference != author statement

evidence aggregation != belief formation

CommonEvidenceState != truth state

The bridge transports evidence.

It does not decide truth.

---

## Inputs

ScientificEvidenceBridge consumes:

1. ScientificDocumentState
2. ScientificStructureState
3. ScientificRelationshipGraph

All three states must refer to the same document.

Document identity mismatch is rejected.

Optional integration context may include:

- scene_id
- session_id
- timestamp

---

## Output

The bridge produces:

ScientificEvidenceBridgeResult

containing:

- document_id
- scene_id
- EvidenceBundle
- schema_version

Schema version:

scientific-evidence-bridge-v0.1

The EvidenceBundle contains documentary EvidenceItem objects
compatible with the existing AISTHESIS evidence architecture.

---

## Documentary Evidence

All evidence created by the bridge uses:

EvidenceSource.DOCUMENTARY

The bridge does not represent paper content as direct visual,
physical, sensory, temporal, or physics evidence.

This preserves source identity.

---

## Structure Mapping

Scientific structure candidates are mapped conservatively.

RESEARCH_QUESTION
→ unknown

HYPOTHESIS
→ unknown

METHOD
→ method

VARIABLE
→ measurement

DATASET
→ dataset_reference

RESULT
→ reported_result

CLAIM
→ author_claim

LIMITATION
→ limitation

UNKNOWN
→ unknown

These mappings describe the epistemic category of extracted
document content.

They do not verify that the content is scientifically correct.

---

## Reported Results

A ResultCandidate becomes:

reported_result

It does not become:

observed

A statement such as:

"We found that X improves Y."

means that the document reports such a result.

The bridge does not establish that:

- the experiment was correctly performed,
- the analysis was correct,
- the result reproduces,
- the result generalizes,
- X actually improves Y.

---

## Author Claims

A ClaimCandidate becomes:

author_claim

An author claim remains distinct from:

- reported measurement
- reproduced result
- AISTHESIS inference
- established fact

CommonEvidenceState preserves this distinction.

---

## Hypotheses

A HypothesisCandidate is transported with:

unknown

epistemic status.

The bridge does not interpret the presence of a hypothesis as
evidence supporting that hypothesis.

---

## Relationship Evidence

ScientificRelationshipCandidate objects are generated by
AISTHESIS.

Therefore relationship candidates enter the evidence architecture
as:

aisthesis_inference

This includes graph vocabulary such as:

- SUPPORTS
- CONTRADICTS
- TESTED_BY
- USES
- MEASURES
- ABOUT
- SUPPORTED_BY

The relationship type remains available inside structured evidence
metadata.

However, it is not promoted to verified evidence semantics.

---

## SUPPORTS Safety Boundary

A graph relationship such as:

Result
→ SUPPORTS
→ Hypothesis

does not populate:

EvidenceItem.supports

and does not populate:

EvidenceItem.supporting_evidence_ids

The relationship remains:

aisthesis_inference

with:

confidence = None

and:

verification_status = not_verified

This prevents graph vocabulary from silently becoming epistemic
support.

---

## CONTRADICTS Safety Boundary

Likewise:

Result
→ CONTRADICTS
→ Hypothesis

does not populate:

EvidenceItem.contradicts

or:

EvidenceItem.opposing_evidence_ids

A candidate contradiction remains an AISTHESIS inference until a
later reasoning or evaluation stage explicitly establishes a
different status.

---

## Confidence

The bridge does not manufacture truth confidence.

EvidenceItem confidence remains:

None

for structure candidates and relationship candidates produced by
v0.1.

Candidate extraction confidence, if present upstream, is preserved
inside structured metadata.

It is not promoted into EvidenceItem truth confidence.

Canonical distinction:

extractor confidence != scientific truth probability

relationship confidence != hypothesis support probability

---

## Provenance

The bridge preserves provenance including:

- document identity
- candidate identity
- relationship identity
- source document elements
- page number where applicable
- extraction method
- relationship inference method
- session context
- epistemic source layer

Evidence is therefore traceable back through the Mini-Lab pipeline.

Documentary EvidenceItem

## CommonEvidenceState Integration

The EvidenceBundle produced by ScientificEvidenceBridge can enter
the existing CommonEvidenceState architecture.

The documentary family remains:

documentary

and its availability can be represented through the existing
CommonEvidenceState availability inventory.

CommonEvidenceState preserves the individual EvidenceItem
epistemic statuses.

For example:

reported_result

remains:

reported_result

author_claim

remains:

author_claim

aisthesis_inference

remains:

aisthesis_inference

The integration does not collapse these categories.

---

## Truth Decision

Scientific Evidence Bridge declares:

truth_decision = not_performed

CommonEvidenceState independently preserves its existing:

truth_decision = not_performed

integrity contract.

Therefore the complete bridge does not perform belief formation or
truth adjudication.

---

## Coordinate Frames

Documentary evidence is not frame-bound physical evidence.

Scientific document evidence therefore does not require a physical
coordinate frame.

The bridge does not fabricate:

- camera coordinates
- world coordinates
- metric geometry
- physical sensor frames

for scientific-document content.

---

## Temporal Integrity

Documentary EvidenceItem timestamps participate in the existing
CommonEvidenceState temporal-integrity checks.

Evidence declared later than the CommonEvidenceState integration
cutoff is rejected.

The bridge therefore does not bypass existing future-evidence
protections.

---

## Determinism

Scientific Evidence Bridge v0.1 uses deterministic evidence
identity.

Given identical inputs and integration context, it is designed to
produce identical EvidenceItem identities and equivalent
EvidenceBundle content.

No random UUID or wall-clock identity is required.

---

## Explicit Non-Goals

Scientific Evidence Bridge v0.1 does NOT:

- verify scientific claims
- reproduce experimental results
- verify statistical analyses
- verify hypotheses
- establish scientific consensus
- calculate truth probability
- perform belief formation
- automatically support hypotheses
- automatically contradict hypotheses
- convert graph edges into verified evidence
- perform literature search
- compare papers
- interpret citations externally
- run experiments
- modify EvidenceItem contracts
- modify EvidenceBundle contracts
- modify CommonEvidenceState
- modify Reasoner Orchestration
- modify Dynamic Reasoning Connectome
- modify live_app.py
- use an LLM
- download a neural model

---

## Relationship to Existing Evidence Architecture

Scientific Evidence Bridge is an adapter.

It does not replace:

EvidenceItem

EvidenceBundle

or:

CommonEvidenceState

The existing evidence architecture remains authoritative for
common evidence transport.

The bridge only converts Mini-Lab artifacts into compatible
documentary evidence.

---

## Relationship to Reasoning

14P.4 ends at CommonEvidenceState integration.

It does not yet ask reasoners to decide:

- whether a claim is supported,
- whether a result is reliable,
- whether a hypothesis survives,
- whether two findings contradict,
- whether evidence should change belief.

Those operations belong to later Mini-Lab reasoning and evaluation
stages.
